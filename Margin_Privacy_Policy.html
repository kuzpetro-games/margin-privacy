MARGIN FOR ANDROID

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026
Developer: Petro Kuz
App: Margin — Tic Tac Toe
Package: com.petrokuz.margin

What stays on your device

Margin is an offline tic-tac-toe game. It stores your current round, scores, win streaks, game mode, difficulty and sound preference locally on your device. This information is used only to continue your game and remember your settings. The app does not send this information to the developer or to a server.

No accounts, tracking or ads

The Android app does not ask you to create an account. It contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking SDKs. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or advertising identifier. The app does not request Android internet access and does not collect or share personal data through gameplay.

Purchases and Google Play

If you buy the app through Google Play, Google processes the purchase under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your payment-card details. Google may provide the developer with store transaction records, ratings, test feedback and platform reports. Those services are separate from the offline game's local storage.

Support messages

If you choose to email the developer, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use that information to respond and resolve the request, retain it only as needed for support or applicable obligations, and do not use it for advertising. Please do not send payment-card details or other sensitive information.

Retention and deletion

Local game data stays on the device until you clear Margin's app storage or uninstall it. This removes your saved round, preferences and scores. The app does not provide cloud sync, and Android automatic backup is disabled. You can contact the developer to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable retention obligations. There is no in-app account to delete.

Children

The game's data handling is the same for every player. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children through gameplay. A parent or guardian may contact the developer about a support message containing a child's personal information.

Changes and contact

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated. Privacy and support contact: kuzpetro@gmail.com.
